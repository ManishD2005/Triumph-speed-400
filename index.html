My back

This is why I want the Speed 400, not a cheaper bike.

  • I have a back issue. I’ll ride every day for years.
  • The Speed 400 has a mono-shock rear suspension. I’ve ridden it many times and it works for me.
  • It’s better for my back than the dual shocks on the Bonneville.
  • Scooters and stiffer bikes would make my back worse over time.
  • That means doctor visits, physio, missed days, and buying another bike early.
  • Good suspension now saves money later, and it protects my health.

I know money

  • I’ve invested ₹4.5L so far.
  • I understand compounding, risk and returns.
  • I compared the bike with every other option using real numbers. The bike wins.
